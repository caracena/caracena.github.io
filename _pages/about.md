Professor at the Adolfo Ibáñez University Business School and Associate Researcher at GobLab UAI. He holds a PhD in Systems Engineering from the University of Chile. He also holds a Master's degree in Information Technology and a Master's degree in Information Technology Administration from the University of Sydney, Australia. He holds a degree in Industrial Civil Engineering from the University of Chile. He is a professor and academic director of the Diploma in AI Tools for Public Policy and the AI ​​and Data Science Project Formulation course at the UAI School of Government. He was a project engineer at the Center for Mathematical Modeling at the University of Chile and a software engineer at Data61 (CSIRO) in Australia. He is co-author of the publication "Responsible Use of AI for Public Policy: Project Formulation Manual" published by the Inter-American Development Bank.
